Book ahead at cataratasdoiguacu.com.br — ~R$131/adult (foreign visitors). Open Mon–Fri 9am–4pm, weekends 8:30am–4pm. Quietest 9–9:30am or 2–4pm.

Tipping & money norms

  • Restaurants: 10% "serviço" is usually already added — check before tipping again.
  • No service charge shown? 10% is polite, not obligatory.
  • Taxis/rideshare: round up, no expected tip.
  • Hotel porters: R$5–10 per bag.
  • Small vendors/street food: carry small BRL notes.
  • Foz park entries & official taxis: card usually fine.
